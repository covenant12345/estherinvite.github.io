How the evening goes

About three hours, unhurried.

  1. Welcome. Drinks, small bites and a short prayer.
  2. The reading. All ten chapters, each of us reading our portion aloud.
  3. The banquet. We eat, the way Esther hosted her own two feasts.
  4. The conversation. Questions, stories and honest answers.
  5. Sending portions. Gift exchange, then we pray for one another.

Your portion to read

Each of us reads one part of the story aloud. Read ahead if you can.

    Sending portions

    Esther 9:22 tells the people to celebrate by "sending portions of food to one another and gifts to the poor." Bring a small treat or gift for one person. Keep it a secret until the night.
